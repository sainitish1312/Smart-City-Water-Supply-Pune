# Smart City Water Supply Project – Pune

## Project Overview

This project presents a project management plan for a proposed Smart City Water Supply and Distribution System in Zone III, Pune. The project focuses on improving water distribution through smart metering, IoT-based monitoring, SCADA integration, leakage detection, stakeholder coordination, procurement planning, and structured project scheduling.

The project was developed as part of the MBA Project Management coursework at GITAM School of Business.

## Project Objectives

- Plan a 24x7 smart water supply and distribution system.
- Develop a structured Work Breakdown Structure (WBS).
- Create phase-wise project schedules and Gantt charts.
- Plan procurement and vendor coordination.
- Integrate IoT devices and SCADA for monitoring and control.
- Identify project stakeholders, risks, constraints, and assumptions.
- Establish communication, quality control, testing, commissioning, and handover processes.
- Develop monitoring and evaluation mechanisms for project performance.

## Project Scope

The proposed project covers approximately 150,000 residences in Zone III, Pune.

Key components include:

- Smart water metering
- IoT-enabled monitoring
- Pressure and leakage detection
- SCADA-based centralized monitoring
- GIS and data integration
- Water distribution infrastructure
- Procurement and vendor management
- Testing and commissioning
- Training and project handover
- Monitoring and evaluation

## Project Management Approach

The project was structured using an eight-phase project management framework:

1. **Project Initiation & Planning**
2. **Design & Engineering**
3. **Procurement**
4. **Construction & Installation**
5. **System Integration – IoT & SCADA**
6. **Testing & Commissioning**
7. **Training & Handover**
8. **Monitoring & Evaluation**

A Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and phase-wise Gantt schedules were developed to organize project activities, dependencies, timelines, and deliverables.

## Technology & Operations

The proposed smart water system incorporates:

- **IoT:** Smart water meters, pressure sensors, and leakage detection devices.
- **SCADA:** Centralized monitoring and control of the water distribution system.
- **GIS:** Mapping and integration of water infrastructure and distribution assets.
- **Data Integration:** Consolidation of operational data for monitoring and decision-making.
- **Cybersecurity:** Consideration of cybersecurity risks associated with connected IoT infrastructure.
